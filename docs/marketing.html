Under the catalog

Orbits You Can Check

SDN-AP is Space Data Network's advanced propagator, built on the entire history of astrodynamics. It keeps the compact element sets and the speed of a mean-element theory, and fits each set to precise orbits, operator ephemerides or observations.

Scored against truth

The published comparison fits SGP4, SGP4-XP and SDN-AP to the same precise orbits and scores each against the same truth. At day 1 and day 7, SDN-AP beats SGP4 in every orbit class, and SGP4-XP in every class except highly elliptical orbits, where the two are close.

The catalog it feeds

We are building SpaceAware's catalog on it: SDN-AP element sets wherever operator or precise data exists, and SGP4 for everything else.

Free to study

SDN-AP is free for non-commercial use. Every cell of the comparison names its count and its build.
