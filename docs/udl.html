Producer brief

Your data already serves the UDL. Offer it to every buyer.

Space Data Network is a storefront with no middlemen: publish the data you already produce, as open Space Data Standards records, from your own node and on your own terms.

What stays the same

Your UDL delivery

Nothing changes in what you deliver to the Unified Data Library. Space Data Network is one more channel for the same products.

Your payloads

The data you already produce maps field for field to Space Data Standards records, the open standard every SDN node reads.

Your terms and keys

You decide what is free, delayed or paid, and your node holds the keys to everything you sell.

Your data types, as SDS records

Every SSA product class that producers deliver has a Space Data Standards record. Records are compact FlatBuffers, read the same way in any language.

Onboarding, step by step

  1. 01

    Run a node

    The SDN desktop app or a server, on your premises or in your cloud. Your node has its own identity key.

  2. 02

    Map your feed

    We map the payloads you already produce, as UDL JSON or CCSDS KVN and XML, to SDS records with you.

  3. 03

    Publish

    Your node signs every record with its key and publishes it content-addressed, so any buyer can check who published it and that nothing changed on the way.

  4. 04

    Set your terms

    Free, delayed, subscription or one-time. Paid products ship encrypted; the content key stays with your node, and a buyer's entitlement unlocks it.

  5. 05

    Show your accuracy

    SDN measures orbit products against independent precise orbits, as its catalog whitepaper does, so accurate data shows it.

Why producers publish on SDS

Very low overhead

Selling runs itself.

Smart contracts take each payment and settle it to your wallet. Your node checks each buyer's entitlement and releases the content key automatically. You run no billing system, customer portal or per-customer integration, so each new subscriber adds almost nothing to your costs.

  1. Buyer paysA subscription or a one-time purchase
  2. Smart contract settlesPayment lands in your wallet
  3. Your node releases the keyAutomatically, on a valid entitlement
  4. Buyer decryptsThe records you published

One protocol

One open format and one network for every buyer, instead of a portal, a login, an API and a format per customer.

Efficient at volume

Binary records sized for constellation ephemerides, with the uncertainty and its calibration label carried in the record.

Provenance by default

Signed at the source and content-addressed, so a record's origin and integrity travel with it to every buyer.
