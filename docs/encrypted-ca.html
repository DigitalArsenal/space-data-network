Encrypted conjunction assessment

Private screening

Compute collision risk on encrypted satellite positions. Nobody sees your orbit, and everyone stays safe.

Why it’s needed: how collision avoidance works →

Why operators don’t share their best data today

National security

Classified orbits reveal capabilities, coverage gaps and mission intent to adversaries.

Commercial IP

Constellation geometry, station-keeping strategy and coverage patterns are multi-billion-dollar trade secrets.

Maneuver ephemeris

Planned maneuvers can be the most sensitive data an operator holds. Encrypted CA screens maneuver ephemeris without broadcasting to competitors that burns, timing or intent are changing.

Liability exposure

Sharing creates obligations: if you share ephemeris and a conjunction is missed, you may carry more liability than if you had shared nothing.

How homomorphic encryption changes that

Math on ciphertext

The network computes d² = Δx² + Δy² + Δz² entirely on encrypted data. The math works; the data stays locked.

Only distance revealed

Only the assessor can decrypt the result, and the result is a distance, not a position. Neither orbit is ever exposed.

Private direct streams

Encrypted ephemeris flows over direct authenticated libp2p streams to a mutually chosen assessor, never over public GossipSub.

Operators send encrypted ephemeris to the assessor, which computes distance on ciphertext with Microsoft SEAL (BFV scheme). Ciphertexts never touch the public network.

Why it matters

The security and operational implications for the space industry.

Classified satellites can take part

Military and intelligence satellites can join global conjunction assessment without compromising operational security.

Protects the investment

Operators keep their most valuable IP, the precise positions behind their competitive advantage, while space stays safe.

No signaled intent

Planned maneuvers and avoidance burns are screened without revealing timing, vectors or intent. The assessor sees risk, not the plan.

Built into FlatBuffers

Mark fields with the he_encrypted schema attribute; the toolchain handles keys, ciphertext serialization and homomorphic operations.

Real time over P2P

Assessments run continuously as new encrypted positions arrive, not as a periodic batch gated by a central coordinator.

Microsoft SEAL BFV

SEAL v4.1.1, BFV with polynomial degree 4096: 128-bit security with about 2 KB of ciphertext per encrypted value.

This is the minimum foundation for secure space traffic management. Without privacy-preserving computation, global coordination requires operators to trust a central authority with their most sensitive data, which most will not do.

Anti-probing defenses

What stops an adversary from spamming fake ephemeris to triangulate hidden satellites? Defense in depth.

Private data path

Ciphertexts flow only over direct authenticated streams to the assessor. HE math is permissionless, so public ciphertexts would invite probing.

Identity staking

Identities post a bond or build reputation before joining assessments. Misbehavior is slashed, making Sybil attacks costly.

Rate limiting

Each identity gets a limited number of submissions and requests per epoch, scaled by reputation and stake.

Threshold-only output

Assessments return SAFE or ALERT, not a distance. Precise distance is disclosed only when both parties opt in after an alert.

Anomaly detection

Grid-spaced positions, systematic sweeps and non-Keplerian ephemeris are flagged, with stake forfeited.

Differential privacy

Calibrated Laplace noise is added before the threshold comparison, so repeated queries cannot triangulate a target.
