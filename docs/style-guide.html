Style Guide

Logo, color, typography and components for anything made with or about Space Data Network. Free to use.

1 · Logo

The Mark

Mark SVG · Lockup SVG
Mark SVG · Lockup SVG
Faint ringThe Earth and the network around it
Amber dotThe satellite being tracked, at top center
Amber tailIts orbit, fading in from 6 o’clock
Three linked nodesPeers exchanging digitally signed data

Rules

  • Use the SVG files. Never redraw, stretch, rotate or re-proportion the mark.
  • The satellite and its tail are always amber, the tail fading from transparent at 6 o’clock to full amber at the satellite. Everything else takes the background variant’s ink.
  • Minimum size 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print. Clear space: one quarter of the mark’s width on every side.
  • No gradients, shadows, outlines, glows, badges or other colors.
  • Write “Space Data Network” in full in headings and titles. “SDN” is fine in body copy after first use, in the product and in code.

2 · Color

Dark First, One Accent

Near-black surfaces, off-white text and amber. Supporting colors each do one job.

Ink on light#1d1d1f · light surfacesText and mark on white

Rules

  • One accent. No other brand hues.
  • Never amber text on light backgrounds; use Amber on light or ink.
  • Red means danger, always with an icon or a word.
  • Text over imagery sits on a panel or carries the standard shadow. Buttons never do.

4 · Layout and components

Pills, Panels And Eyebrows

Primary Secondary Hover me
  • Content width 1120 px, reading columns 720–860 px, 20 px gutters, 72 px section padding, centered pages.
  • Radii: 20 px cards, 12 px panels, images and tables, 18 px diagram frames, pills for buttons.
  • Cards: translucent panel, hairline border, backdrop blur. Icons: 24 px line icons, 1.7 px stroke, amber.
  • Buttons: the hovered button turns white with black text; the primary gives up its white meanwhile.
  • Every section: eyebrow, Title Case heading, a one- or two-sentence lede. No horizontal scroll at 390 px.
